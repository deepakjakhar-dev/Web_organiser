# Sitewell Launch Kit

## Positioning
Sitewell helps small businesses keep their website online and sends easy-to-read reports.

## Who to target first
- solo business owners
- freelancers
- small agencies
- service businesses
- small e-commerce stores

## Outreach message
Subject: Free website health reports for small businesses

Hi [Name],

I built a simple tool that helps small businesses see when their website goes down, gets slow, or picks up broken links.

If you want, I can set up one website for free and send you a simple report by email.

No pressure, just thought it might be useful.

Best,
[Your Name]

## LinkedIn post
I built a simple website health tool for small businesses.

It checks uptime, broken links, and basic security, then sends easy-to-read reports by email.

I am looking for a few early users who want to try it free on one website.

## Reddit / Indie Hackers post
I am building a simple website monitoring tool for small businesses.

It focuses on uptime, broken links, and email reports that non-technical owners can actually understand.

Would love feedback from founders, freelancers, and small agency owners.

## First offer
- one free website
- free setup help
- upgrade only if they want multiple sites or PDF reports

## First goal
- get 5 people to try it
- collect 1 or 2 testimonials
- convert the clearest users to Pro
